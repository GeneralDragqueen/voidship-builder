How to use

Every category in the loadout shows what is installed. Change or Add opens a picker listing everything that fits this hull, with the items that do not fit shown below and the reason why. Hover or arrow through a row to see what it would do to your budgets and stats before you install it. ✕ removes a supplemental component; essentials are required and can only be swapped.

Export (or e) builds a full dossier of the ship: stats, budgets, every component with its rules text, weapons, bonuses and complications. Copy it as Discord-ready text (split into message-sized parts) or Markdown, or print it to PDF. If this viewer blocks printing or the clipboard, the text appears in a box you can select and copy by hand; the offline copy of this file prints normally.

Sources

Core Rulebook Chapter VIII (pp. 194–208) with the official Errata applied: Ryza Pattern Plasma Battery draws 8 Power, every Additional Facility is once per ship, the Titanforge Lance Battery fits Light Cruisers and Cruisers only, and a weapon's Strength caps the hits of lances as well as macrobatteries. Components from other sourcebooks (Into the Storm, Battlefleet Koronus) are not included.

Saving

Builds autosave in the browser you are using, tied to the page's address: a downloaded copy of the file, the artifact link, and each device keep separate lists. To carry builds across, use Copy backup code or Download backup in the Builds panel and Restore backup… on the other side; restoring skips builds that are already there. A single ship travels as a share code or link.

Budgets

Space comes from the hull, Power from the plasma drive, Ship Points from your Warrant roll. None may overflow at creation. Leftover Ship Points are added to your starting Profit Factor (p. 207). After creation only Space, Power and Profit Factor matter. Spare Power has no use in play under the Core rules: damage makes components unpowered but never lowers what the drive generates. Its only value is headroom for components you buy later.
